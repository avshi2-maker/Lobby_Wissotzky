Background · What Went Wrong

The existing floor had two fundamental problems — and both are fixable.

The white floor installed three years ago has worn down far beyond the rate expected from a luxury building. The causes are documented and clear, and this proposal is built precisely to avoid repeating them.

01

Wrong Material Category

The existing product — a flexible epoxy-acrylic resin — was selected in a category not suited for continuous public foot traffic in a luxury building. Its inherent flexibility is precisely what causes the scratching and staining. A luxury building requires an entirely different category: a rigid cementitious microtopping.

02

Unverified Adhesion

The resin sits on top of the original ceramic tiles, with no documentation of an adhesion test between the two layers. This is the likely cause of the phenomena you see today. In this proposal, that test will be performed by an independent certified institute — before we start any work.

The Solution · Topcret Baxab

The system installed in hotel lobbies, museums and boutiques across Europe.

Baxab is the flagship microtopping system from Topcret — the Spanish manufacturer that invented the category (Barcelona, 2005). The coating is supplied directly from Barcelona, without intermediaries and without rebranding. The system is engineered for surfaces that must stay beautiful under continuous public traffic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What the residents are asking.

Can I enter the building normally during the works?
Yes. The project is built in two phases (A and B), and at any given moment only half the lobby is closed. Building entrance, elevators, mail access and parking access continue to operate normally.
Why wait for the test report before final approval?
The test report (Phase 1, ~€4,000) is a low-cost, limited-commitment first step — but it formally determines whether the existing substrate can receive the new system. This is precisely the step that was skipped in the previous installation.
Why is pure white problematic? We want a bright lobby.
The manufacturer explicitly recommends slightly warm tones (Lino, Niebla) over pure white, to balance aesthetic brightness against long-term stain visibility. Both alternatives appear fully bright in a well-lit lobby while concealing scratches and minor staining.
What is the warranty? What happens if something fails?
SAPIRIM provides an installation warranty of 24 months from final handover. In addition, Topcret provides a 3-year manufacturer warranty (Garantía de Coste Cero). Dedicated third-party project insurance is included in the proposal.
Why not choose a cheaper solution?
You already had a cheaper solution — and that is the current status. The wrong material category is the reason for the current failure. Baxab is not an "upgrade" — it is the correct category for this type of building. The budget gap dissolves entirely when you look at the lifetime — 8-12 years instead of 3.
Who checks quality during execution?
Avshi Sapir is present on site in daily intervals during installation phases. Ongoing quality control — mix ratios, layer thickness, temperature and humidity conditions — is documented in writing. At the end of each phase, a joint inspection is performed with the building management representative.
How is the floor maintained over time?
At project completion you receive a written maintenance manual in Hebrew, an initial supply of manufacturer-approved detergent, and two training sessions (in Hebrew) for the building's cleaning crew. In addition, 3 months of post-handover industrial cleaning service is included.
When does the actual work start?
On-site work begins in Week 4 from formal proposal authorization. Weeks 1-3 are dedicated to substrate testing, materials ordering from Barcelona, and color approval on sample panels. Total — 9 weeks from authorization to final handover.
